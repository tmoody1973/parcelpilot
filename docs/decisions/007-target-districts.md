# 007 — First-slice districts: LB1, LB2, LB3, RB1, RB2; LB1 is the demo district

**Date:** 2026-09-21 · **Status:** decided by Tarik (product owner); expert reviewer confirmation still open · **Decided by:** Tarik

**Decision.** The first vertical slice supports Milwaukee's Local Business (LB1, LB2, LB3) and Regional Business (RB1, RB2) districts from Chapter 295 subchapter 6. LB1 is the demo district.

**Why this came up.** The plan says two to four districts, chosen where small multifamily infill actually happens. Picking wrong means a polished product nobody's deals fall into.

**Options.**
1. Residential multi-family districts (RM1–RM7). Cost: apartments-only story; no ground-floor retail case, which is half the PRD demo.
2. LB and RB commercial corridors (chosen). Cost: five codes instead of two to four, though they share one use table (295-603-1) and one design-standards table (295-605-2), so the extra districts are extra columns, not extra tables.
3. A mix of one RM and one LB. Cost: two subchapters to ingest and review before the first demo.

**What we chose and why.** Option 2. Mixed-use over retail is the infill product Milwaukee corridors want, and LB1's 45 ft height cap and 1,200 sq ft per unit make the PRD's demo concept (46 ft, 24 units on a 7,000 sq ft lot) fail in two visible, explainable ways, while the same concept passes on an LB2 corridor lot. One table, two outcomes.

**What we gave up.** Any residential-district coverage in v1, and the narrower "two to four" scope the PRD asked for.

**How we'll know if this was right.** Pilot sites (M6) land in these districts at least half the time; if most candidate sites are RM, expand there next.
